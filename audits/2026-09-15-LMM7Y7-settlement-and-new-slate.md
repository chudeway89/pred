## Transcription correction

The original LMM7Y7 leg was **Newcastle +1 Asian at 1.33**. It was previously misreported as Leeds +1. Because Leeds won 4–1, the actual original leg lost. The previously described “2.88 four-leg recommendation” cannot be treated as an observed or valid ticket: it relied on the wrong team name and therefore has no legitimate settlement record.

## Root-cause review

1. **The code was not snapshotted immutably.** A later transcription reversed the team on the first handicap.
2. **Thin-data markets were overused.** Reserve/U21 matches, second-half 12 markets, excluded totals and corner totals created settlement and modelling uncertainty.
3. **Correlation was ignored.** Multiple over-goals markets relied on the same broad narrative without independent probability estimates.
4. **Market mechanics were not preserved.** Asian totals and custom goal-band labels require exact screenshots or raw contract text.
5. **Headline odds dominated selection quality.** Forty legs at roughly 216,971 odds were structurally incompatible with the analytical five-leg ceiling.
